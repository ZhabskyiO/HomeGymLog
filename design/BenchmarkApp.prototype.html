1Benchmark — home-gym program builder & tracker (iOS)

Brief. A personal training app for one athlete with dumbbells (≤55 lb), a bench and a Marcy 150 lb home gym. It generates a 1–6 month program (3/4/5 days a week), phased Foundation → Build → Peak → Deload, and tracks sets, PRs and streaks. Swipe right on a set to log it, left to adjust/skip. Rest timer auto-starts.

Assumptions. Split is picked from frequency: 3 = full body A/B/C, 4 = upper/lower, 5 = push/pull/legs + upper/lower. Weights step +5 lb per week (capped at 55 lb dumbbells, 150 lb stack); deload week is −30%. Units in lb. DevDigest used only for vibe (dark-first, tight type, one saturated accent). Use Tweaks to switch theme, jump to a mid-program state, or trigger the missed-day prompt.
