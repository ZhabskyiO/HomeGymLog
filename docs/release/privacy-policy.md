# HomeGymLog privacy policy

HomeGymLog stores everything on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Your program, your logged sets, your notes and your settings never leave your iPhone unless you choose to export them.

The app collects no data. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

A backup file you export leaves your device only when you choose to share it, and only to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet. The developer never receives it.

Reminders and the rest-timer alert are local notifications scheduled on your device. The Lock Screen rest timer is a Live Activity drawn on your device. Neither uses a push server.

Deleting the app deletes its data. The app keeps nothing anywhere else.
